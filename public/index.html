FIELD MANUAL

Defend the core.

Enemies follow the illuminated road. Stop them before they reach the core at its end.

  1. Choose Pulse, Mortar, or Cryo below the map.
  2. Tap an empty pad to build. Roads stay clear.
  3. Tap Start wave. Towers fire automatically.
  4. Tap a built tower to upgrade or sell.

Pulse fires fast. Mortar hits groups and ignores armor. Cryo slows enemies. Mix them for the best defense.

Bosses arrive every fourth wave. Survive all 12 waves to unlock the next sector. Build and upgrade during waves, or take your time between them.
